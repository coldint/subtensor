---
title: difficulty
description: Current PoW registration difficulty; u64::MAX means PoW registration is effectively disabled.
---

`difficulty` is the proof-of-work target a miner's registration nonce must
beat to claim a UID via PoW registration. It is a raw u64: finding a valid
nonce takes about `difficulty` hash attempts on average, so the value is a
direct price in compute. Miners planning PoW registration care about it;
`u64::MAX` is the sentinel for "no nonce can ever pass" — PoW registration
effectively disabled, burned registration only.

## How it works

The new `pow_register` call checks a domain-separated seal binding the subnet,
recent block hash, hotkey, signing coldkey and nonce. Its little-endian 256-bit
hash multiplied by `difficulty` must not overflow, so success has probability
roughly one in `difficulty`. A zero configured value is treated as one.
At `u64::MAX`, mining is effectively impractical.

PoW must be enabled by the subnet owner and general registration must be open.
The legacy `register` call remains burn-based. The new PoW call is fee-free for
direct zero-tip submission, and charges neither burn nor initial collateral.

The automatic difficulty controller remains retired. The current stored
`difficulty` is the admission threshold until root changes it. The historical
floor and ceiling fields do not automatically adjust this threshold. The
faucet keeps its separate fixed difficulty and seal format.

## Reading and setting

```
btcli sudo get --netuid N --name difficulty
```

Root/governance only:
[`sudo_set_difficulty`](/code/pallets/admin-utils/src/lib.rs#L827-L841)
in the AdminUtils pallet
requires the root origin and takes a raw u64. It is not settable by the
subnet owner via `btcli sudo set`.
